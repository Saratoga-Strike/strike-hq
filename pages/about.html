How Strike Started

Strike started with three best friends who had talked for years about building a business together.

The goal was simple: build a company that helps everyday people save money on services they already use while creating real opportunities for the people who work here.

We saw families paying more than they needed to for home internet and mobile service, and we saw talented people looking for careers with better training, growth, and upside.

That idea became Strike.

Today our mission is to help customers lower their monthly bills while building a workplace where motivated people can learn, grow, and create a better future for themselves.

The three of us have always been close enough to feel like brothers, but what matters most is the team we're building and the customers we get to serve.
